I’m Austin A Smith. I work across systems administration and software design, with a focus on making everyday tools easier to understand and use.

HOW THE WORK CONNECTS

Useful on the screen.
Practical behind it.

A project can begin with a repeating task: preparing an invoice, making a worksheet, or keeping track of a small team’s workday. I use those tasks to guide what I build.

I care about the complete experience—how someone starts, where their work is saved, how the app is installed, and what happens when they need an export or a backup.

These are independent projects, developed one useful improvement at a time.

01 / START WITH THE JOB

Understand the actual task.

Build around the people doing the work and the outcome they need.

02 / KEEP IT UNDERSTANDABLE

Make ownership clear.

Keep local storage, permissions, exports, and backups understandable.

03 / SHOW THE CURRENT STATE

Make the work reviewable.

Be clear about demos, work in progress, and available releases.
