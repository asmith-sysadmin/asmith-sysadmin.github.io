Austin can help you from here. Download the support app, open it, and approve his connection.

1. Download the support app

Choose your computer below. Downloads come directly from RustDesk, the app NorthStar uses for remote help.

I'm on a Mac

Click the Apple menu at the top left, then About This Mac. Does it say Apple or Intel?

Download for Apple chipDownload for Intel chip

Not sure? Call Austin and he'll help you choose.

I'm on Windows

Use this download for most Windows computers.

Download for Windows

Have a Windows computer with an ARM chip? Ask Austin for the right download.

2. Open it

Open the file you downloaded. On a Mac, drag RustDesk into Applications, then open RustDesk from there. On Windows, open the download and follow the prompts.

My Mac is asking for permission

This lets Austin see your screen and help with your mouse and keyboard.

  1. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  2. Find Screen Recording (sometimes called Screen & System Audio Recording) and turn on RustDesk.
  3. Do the same under Accessibility. If RustDesk asks for Input Monitoring, turn it on there too.
  4. If asked, quit and reopen RustDesk.

Austin can walk you through this on the phone.

3. Let Austin connect

Tell Austin the ID number shown in RustDesk. When his connection request appears, choose Accept.

Stay at your computer while he helps. To finish, disconnect in RustDesk and quit the app.

You're in control. You can end the session whenever you want. You don't need to set up a permanent password.
